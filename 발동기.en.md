# When to reach for which skill (REBORN LABS Skill Pack 2)

> This section was added by the **REBORN LABS Skill Pack**.
> The skills themselves belong to their authors and **we did not change a single character** of them.
> All that is written here is "when this comes up, open that one first" — this text is the only part we wrote.
>
> **Why it is here** — on 2026-09-05 we measured it. Five installed skills were **never once invoked
> across three runs.** Having a skill installed and having it opened are different things.
> With the sentences below in place, all three runs invoked them.
>
> Don't want it? Delete from the top marker to the bottom one.
> The installed skills keep working exactly the same.

## Writing, changing, or simplifying code

**Call `karpathy-guidelines` first.** Before touching the code, not after.
It sets the posture — strip the over-engineering, make the cut surgical.

## Reading and judging someone else's code (pre-merge review)

**Call `code-review-and-quality` first.** Before writing even one line of the review.
If the job is flattening layers rather than finding defects, look at `code-simplification` alongside it.

Applies to — code review · pre-merge checks · "take a look at this" · "anything wrong here?" · hunting bugs.

## Building a screen (landing page, app UI, portfolio)

**Call `design-taste-frontend` first.** Before writing any HTML or CSS.
The moment right before that is exactly where templated-looking screens come from.

For motion work — gestures, springs, sheets, momentum — call `apple-design`.
★That is a **motion** skill. You don't need it for color and typography alone.

## Removing the AI tell from writing

**Call `humanizer`.** It leaves the meaning alone and makes the sentences sound like a person wrote them.
(In our measurements this one got invoked reliably even without a trigger line. It's noted here for completeness.)

## Working with Google Analytics or traffic data

**Call `analytics__google-analytics-data-api-basics` first.**
Traffic, sessions and conversion queries · GA4 properties · visitors by channel.
What you need for authentication is documented inside that skill.

★**If the data won't come, do not invent numbers.** Write down what was missing that blocked you.
(Without a Google account, GCP credentials and a GA4 property ID, the query cannot run. That is normal.)

## How to finish work

Calling the right skill and **finishing the job** are both required.

- If information is missing, ask. But **don't ask and then stop** —
  make the most plausible assumption, carry the work through to the end,
  and **tell me what you assumed.**
- Whatever file was asked for, leave it behind. That is what finishing means.
- If something a skill needs isn't available in this environment, build the structure that skill
  was going to set up by hand. "The infrastructure wasn't there" is not an answer.
